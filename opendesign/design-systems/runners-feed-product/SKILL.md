# Runners Feed product UI

Use the production frontend as the source of truth. Preserve its dark athletic
character, acid-lime status accent, square panels, and divider-led hierarchy.

- Use `tokens/colors_and_type.css` for color and type decisions.
- Prefer grids, rules, and whitespace over shadows or decorative cards.
- Keep corners square except for circular progress markers.
- Use uppercase English only for short structural labels. User guidance and
  actions are concise Korean.
- Use the accent for active state, progress, primary actions, and key measured
  values—not for large background areas.
- Maintain at least 44px interaction targets on small screens.
- Do not introduce gradients beyond the existing restrained background glow,
  extra accent colors, or emoji iconography.
