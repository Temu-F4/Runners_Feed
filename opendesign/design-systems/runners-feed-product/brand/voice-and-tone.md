# Voice and tone

- Address the runner directly in concise Korean.
- State the current action or outcome before implementation detail.
- Use polite `-습니다` wording for progress and completion messages.
- Use short noun phrases for field labels and navigation.
- Keep technical English for established system terms such as Job ID, Status,
  MP4, Object Storage, and model names.
- Write numbers with their unit next to the value: `175cm`, `68%`, `250 MiB`.
- Do not use emoji, exclamation-heavy copy, slogans inside workflow controls, or
  vague success language.
- Error messages say what failed and, where possible, what the runner can do.
